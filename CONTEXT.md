# RRB NTPC Mock Tests

A candidate creates private English-language mock tests to practise for RRB NTPC Undergraduate using provided study material and, when needed, online sources.

## Language

**Candidate**:
The person preparing for RRB NTPC Undergraduate who provides study material and takes private mock tests.
_Avoid_: Student, user

**Study Material**:
A candidate-provided PDF, DOCX, or group of images containing content to be assessed, such as notes or a book.
_Avoid_: Source document, upload

**Source Selection**:
The part of one Study Material that a candidate chooses for a Topic Test or Weekly Test, such as a chapter or page range.
_Avoid_: Scope, chunk

**Topic**:
An area of the RRB NTPC syllabus that the Candidate studies, whether or not they have provided Study Material for it.

**Past-Paper Question (PYQ)**:
A question attributed to an earlier RRB NTPC examination, with its origin identified as an official paper, a third-party copy, or a memory-based reconstruction.
_Avoid_: Official question, unless the origin is verified

**Practice Question**:
A new question written to follow the relevant Past-Paper Question pattern.
_Avoid_: PYQ

**Mock Test**:
A timed set of Past-Paper Questions and Practice Questions that a Candidate submits for a score and subsequent review.
_Avoid_: Quiz

**Topic Test**:
A Mock Test covering one Topic, using a Source Selection when available and approved online sources as needed.

**Full Mock**:
A Mock Test matching the complete RRB NTPC Undergraduate CBT-1 blueprint: 100 questions across the three exam sections in 90 minutes.

**Weekly Test**:
A Mock Test taken at the end of the Candidate's study week, covering the topics the Candidate marked as studied that week, with new questions on weak topics and Retry Questions at the end.

**Weak Topic**:
A Topic in which the Candidate has recently answered questions incorrectly often enough to warrant more practice.

**Retry Question**:
An exact question the Candidate answered incorrectly in the previous Weekly Test, shown as an untimed, separately reported extra at the end of the next Weekly Test.
_Avoid_: New practice question

**Source Reference**:
The location in Study Material or an approved online source that supports a factual question and its answer, shown during Answer Review.
_Avoid_: Citation

**Worked Solution**:
The calculation or reasoning that establishes the answer to a self-contained Mathematics or Reasoning question.

**Answer Review**:
The post-submission view of a Mock Test's correct answers, explanations, and Source References.

**Question Flag**:
The Candidate's report that a question or answer is wrong or ambiguous; a flagged question is excluded from the Candidate's score and future tests.
