# Label the origin of online past-paper questions

The local tool may use approved third-party RRB NTPC papers because official question-paper access is generally offered through time-limited candidate links rather than a stable public archive. Each imported question retains its source and is labelled as an official paper, a third-party claimed copy, or a memory-based reconstruction; only verified official material receives the official label. This allows online PYQ practice while making uncertainty in third-party collections visible.
